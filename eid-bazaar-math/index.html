    👨‍👩‍👧 For grown-ups

    Eid Bazaar Math practises addition for ages 5–8 inside an Eid story.

    • ➕
      Skills
      Sums to 5, to 10 and to 20 · adding tens · two-digit sums without regrouping.
    • 👆
      How it plays
      Children tap real objects to count, then pick or type the answer.
    • 🤗
      Gentle
      Wrong answers are never punished — the game counts together with the child and lets them try again.
    • 🧰
      Helpers
      “Count with me”, a number line, and a ten frame / tens-and-ones chart.
    • ⌨️
      Keyboard
      Number keys to type · Enter to check or continue · Backspace to delete.
    • 🔒
      Privacy
      Progress is saved on this device only. No accounts, no ads, no tracking.
