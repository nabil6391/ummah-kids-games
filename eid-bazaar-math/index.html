    For grown-ups

    Eid Bazaar Math practises addition for ages 5–8: sums to 5, to 10 and to 20, adding tens, and two-digit sums without regrouping.

    Children count real objects (tap to count), then pick or type the answer. Wrong answers are never punished — the game counts together with the child and lets them try again. Helpers: “Count with me”, a number line, and a ten frame / tens-and-ones chart.

    Keyboard: number keys to type, Enter to check / continue, Backspace to delete.

    Progress is saved on this device only.
