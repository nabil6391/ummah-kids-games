Who do you want to be? Your sibling will follow you and help!

Use WASD or the arrow keys. Grab with E, / or Enter.

❓ How to Play

Work together to bring Sadaqah (gifts of kindness) to every neighbour before the sun sets for iftar!

🧢 Zaid
WASD move
E grab / give / toss
📱 Left side of the screen
🧕 Amina
↑←↓→ move
/ or Enter grab / give / toss
📱 Right side of the screen
📦Big boxes need BOTH of you. Grab a side each and walk the same way.
🔵Buttons open gates while someone stands on them.
🏗️Lifts go up while someone stands on the green button.
🪵Fences: stand next to one and press grab to toss a parcel over.
🐱Sleepy cats move once you give them water. Be kind to animals!
🌅Sunset: finish before iftar for ⭐⭐⭐. Being late is okay!
