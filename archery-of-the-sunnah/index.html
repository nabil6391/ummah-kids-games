“Learn archery, for it is the best of your games.”

Drag to aim, hold to draw the bow, release to loose the arrow.
Mind the wind! 🌬️

How to Play

🖱️ Aim: move your mouse or finger — the bow follows.

🏹 Draw: press and hold. The power bar swings — time it!

✨ Loose: let go to shoot. Arrows arc with gravity.

🌬️ Wind: watch the windsock — it pushes your arrow sideways.

⭐ Stars: reach the score goal to pass the round and earn coins.

💰 Coins: buy new bows and upgrades in the Armory.

⌨️ Keys: arrows to aim, hold Space to draw.
