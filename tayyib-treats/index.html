👪 For Grown-ups

Tayyib Treats teaches children to choose wholesome food and to be kind to animals. Children drag (or tap) food to each animal. Good food fills the tummy meter; junk food gives a gentle tummy-ache and a hint. There is no way to fail. Read-aloud uses your browser's built-in voice.
