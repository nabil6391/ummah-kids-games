👪 For Grown-ups

Zellige Studio introduces children (about 5–11) to Islamic geometric art — the star-and-rosette patterns that decorate mosques and madrasas from Morocco to India. Filling a tile also fills its symmetric partners, so children feel rotational & mirror symmetry (the dihedral group D4) while they make something beautiful. There is no way to lose and no timer.

Islamic content & sources

    Geometric art is a celebrated, non-figurative Islamic tradition. No living beings are depicted as sacred, and no prophets, angels, Allah or Shaytan are ever shown. Please review with a local scholar if unsure.
