Noor Defenders

The grumpy Gloomies want to dim the masjid's lanterns!
Build good deeds to cheer them up with light.

❓ How to play
  1. 1
    Pick a good deed from the bar at the bottom.
  2. 2
    Tap a green tile next to the path to build it there.
  3. 3
    Press Start Wave. Light from your good deeds cheers up the Gloomies and they float away happy!
  4. 4
    Cheering up Gloomies gives you ✨ Noor. Use it to build more and to upgrade (tap a tower).
  5. 5
    If a Gloomy reaches the masjid, a 🏮 lantern goes dim. Keep them glowing to earn ⭐⭐⭐!

Keys: 1–4 pick a tower · Space next wave · F speed · U upgrade · P pause · M sound
