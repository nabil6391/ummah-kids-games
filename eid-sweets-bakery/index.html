ℹ️ For grown-ups

Eid Sweets Bakery is a gentle baking game for children aged about 5–11. Children follow simple steps (add, stir, shape, bake, decorate, box) and then give their treats to a neighbour. Each gift is followed by a short hadith or verse about sharing and gifts.

Nothing can be failed. If treats get too dark in the oven, the child is kindly asked to try again. There are no ads, no purchases and no accounts. Progress is saved only on this device.

All characters are original cartoon neighbours. No prophets, angels or companions are shown. All ingredients are halal.
