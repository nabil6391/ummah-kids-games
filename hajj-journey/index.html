Hajj Journey

A family board game about the Hajj 🤍

1–4 players · take turns on one device · ages 6–12

Who is going on the journey?

Tap 🧑 / 🤖 to switch a pilgrim between a person and a friendly computer pilgrim.

Bismillah — the journey begins!

How to play
  • 🎲 Roll the die and walk along the road.
  • 🟡 Gold spaces are stations of Hajj. You always stop at the next station and do its activity — in order!
  • ⭐ Green = good deed (move ahead) · ❓ Blue = quiz · 😅 Orange = oops!
  • 🤝 It's not a race — everyone completes every station and earns a certificate.
